User, 2026-10-24: "dont see the super deals. Just focus on sku price." CLAUDE.md 6.6 and change log updated. SuperDeals / Limited Offers on the item page no longer disqualifies a product; cost = SKU List price of the exact variant (read signed in). Struck-through, welcome/new-shopper, coupon and coin prices are still not used.

Clarified 2026-11-04: when the AliPrice chart shows a one-month spike far above the SKU price (night light: chart high 22.99 vs SKU 1.89), the user chose the SKU price as the cost. The chart high is not used. (A "one-time offer, max 1 per shopper" price is different: that is a promo limit, so I treat the struck-through baseline as the cost until the user says otherwise.)

**Why:** most cheap AliExpress items carry a SuperDeals badge on their normal SKU price, so the old rule rejected viable products.
**How to apply:** read the SKU List price and stock; keep 4% FX, 6% buffer, stock 20+/50+. Also on 24 Oct the user said to keep hunting Go Dropship and AliExpress to the 35-product budget each, and to find products through competitor stores and ZIK trending niches.
